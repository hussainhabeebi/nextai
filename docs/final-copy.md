## FAQ

A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

**Good questions. Simple answers.**

Still have one? Let’s talk on WhatsApp ↗

**Do I need to learn software?**

No. Send bills, voice notes and questions on WhatsApp. We take care of your books.

**Is my data safe?**

Your books are kept private, with encrypted data and access limited to authorised people. You approve entries and can export your records.

**Who files my GST/VAT?**

Filing is handled through partner tax professionals on the Pro plan. We confirm the scope, fees and your approval before any filing.

**What if the AI makes a mistake?**

You can review and approve every entry. Flag anything that looks wrong in the chat. A real accountant reviews your books; Pro includes dedicated accountant review and filing support.

**Can my staff use it?**

Yes. The Business and Pro plans include team access, so your staff can send bills while you stay in control.

**How do I cancel?**

Message us on WhatsApp to cancel anytime. You can export your books before you leave. No card is needed for the trial.
