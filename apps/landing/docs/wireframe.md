# Mobile-first wireframe

1. Sticky header: supplied horizontal NextAi logo, language picker, compact Start free button.
2. Hero: small business eyebrow; “Your accounts team on WhatsApp.” headline; plain sub-line; orange trial CTA; secondary scroll link; trust text. Below, an original animated phone/chat illustration with a bill, saved reply and daily summary. Audience strip closes the hero.
3. How it works: three stacked numbered rows with big simple icons, Send / We record / You know. Three columns on desktop.
4. Services: six compact cards, two columns on mobile and three on desktop.
5. Before/after: neutral “Without” card followed by deep teal “With” card; side by side on desktop.
6. Language examples: heading then English message, Malayalam voice-note illustration and Arabic RTL message. Desktop split layout.
7. Human trust: review emblem, short statement and Pro plan clarification.
8. Pricing: India/UAE segmented control, three stacked cards with Business highlighted. Desktop three columns. Explicit placeholder prices.
9. Security: four concise promises in a two-by-two mobile grid.
10. FAQ: six native disclosure rows below a friendly heading.
11. Final CTA: deep teal band, large headline and orange signup button.
12. Footer: stacked supplied logo, contact links, policy links and Aiingo attribution.

Once the hero scrolls away, a bottom mobile CTA stays within thumb reach. Signup opens a bottom sheet on mobile and centered dialog on desktop. It contains only business name, country-coded WhatsApp number and country; local validation precedes API submission. A successful response shows the WhatsApp confirmation. A failed response preserves the form and offers retry/chat. Escape and the close button dismiss the sheet.
